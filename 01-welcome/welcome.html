Bringing together researchers, funders, and stakeholders to discuss the advancements, challenges, and future of large scientific collaborations

  • Three days, October 6–8
  • Fully virtual on Zoom

Welcome everyone. This is our fifth year! Big team science: collaborations where a comparatively large number of researchers pool intellectual and/or material resources toward a common goal. If that describes your work, or you’re just curious, you belong here.
